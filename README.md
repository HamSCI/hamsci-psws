# HamSCI Personal Space Weather Station (PSWS) Working Group

The HamSCI PSWS Working Group builds, deploys, and operates the [HamSCI Personal Space Weather Station network](https://www.hamsci.org/psws): affordable HF, VLF, and ground magnetometer instruments, plus GPS-disciplined WSPRSonde beacons, hosted largely by amateur radio volunteers. It is the lead working group for HamSCI's instrument and observing activities.

- **Website:** https://hamsci.github.io/hamsci-psws/
- **Leads:** Dr. Nathaniel Frissell (W2NAF), The University of Scranton; Dr. Kristina Collins (KD8OXT), Space Science Institute
- **Mailing list:** [hamsci-psws@googlegroups.com](https://groups.google.com/g/hamsci-psws)
- **Chartered:** 1 January 2020, with The University of Scranton's first NSF DASI award (AGS-2002278)
- **Data:** [HamSCI PSWS central database](https://pswsnetwork.eng.ua.edu/)
- **Documentation:** [HamSCI/PSWS_Documentation](https://github.com/HamSCI/PSWS_Documentation)

The group's official record (leads, mission, mailbox, and meeting calendar) lives in the HamSCI working-group registry and is published in the [HamSCI working group directory](https://www.hamsci.org/working-groups). Change it there, by pull request to the registry, and not in this repository.
